## 摘要
预测物体动力学（即世界建模）是机器人操作中的一项基础挑战，而对可变形物体进行建模由于其高维状态空间和复杂材料属性而尤为困难。当前的世界模型主要通过两种不同范式来处理这一问题：在二维像素空间上学习动力学，或在更显式的三维几何空间中建模。然而，由于缺乏多样化的大规模真实世界数据，人们仍难以系统性地理解这两类方法各自的优势与局限。为此，我们提出了 Deform360，一个大规模视觉-触觉数据集，包含198种日常物体、1980段交互序列，以及超过215小时的观测数据。这些数据由41个环绕视角相机和双手触觉夹爪采集，用于同时捕获全局运动与接触引起的局部形变。借助一种新颖的无标记视觉-触觉三维跟踪流程来提取稠密几何与运动信息，我们系统性地评估了当前最先进的世界模型，对比了二维视频模型与三维粒子模型。最后，我们通过在可变形物体上的机器人规划任务进行了初步演示，表明了该数据集在真实世界中的应用潜力。我们的分析揭示了结构先验与可扩展性之间权衡关系的关键见解，为未来可泛化的以可变形物体为中心的世界建模研究提供了坚实的基准。项目主页：https://deform360.lhy.xyz

## Abstract
Predicting object dynamics (i.e., world modeling) is a fundamental challenge for robotic manipulation, and modeling deformable objects presents a particularly difficult case due to their high-dimensional state spaces and complex material properties. While current world models approach this through two distinct paradigms: learning the dynamics over the 2D pixel space or more explicit 3D geometric space. A systematic understanding of their relative strengths and limitations remains elusive due to the lack of diverse, large-scale real-world data. To address this, we present Deform360, a large-scale visuotactile dataset featuring 198 daily-life objects, 1,980 interaction sequences, and over 215 hours of observations from 41 surround-view cameras and bimanual tactile grippers to capture both global motion and contact-induced local deformations. Leveraging a novel markerless visuotactile 3D tracking pipeline to extract dense geometry and motion, we systematically evaluate current state-of-the-art world models, comparing 2D video models against 3D particle models. Finally, we provide a preliminary demonstration indicating the real-world applicability of our dataset by performing robot planning tasks on deformable objects. Our analysis reveals key insights into the trade-offs between structural priors and scalability, providing a solid benchmark for future research in generalizable deformable object-centric world modeling.
